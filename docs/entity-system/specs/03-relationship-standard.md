# Entity Relationship Standard

> **CR-BP-122 Stage 2 output 3.** Normative spec for the General Entity System's relationship discipline in `dea-catalog-business-processes`. Foundation: [00-charter.md](../00-charter.md), [02-normative-design-proposal.md §3](../02-normative-design-proposal.md). Companion: [CR-012 EGV-006](../../../../dea-governance/change-requests/CR-012-org-wide-entity-governance.md).

## 1. Scope

This spec codifies the **relationship discipline** for every governed entity in `dea-catalog-processes`. The discipline is built on CR-012 normative requirement **EGV-006 (Relationship and Reference Integrity)**.

The discipline covers:
- The typed-relationship vocabulary.
- The source / target type constraints per relationship type.
- The cardinality rules per relationship type.
- The cross-catalog reference resolution (deferred to T8 of the umbrella programme).
- The "no competing definitions" rule.

## 2. The canonical relationship types

The catalog instantiates 4 canonical relationship types from the metamodel. The types are the only approved forms; a new type is added by amending this spec (per EGV-006: "A repository SHALL NOT introduce a competing definition of a canonical relationship without an approved normative change").

### RT-001: `composes` (structural composition)

> **Source / target constraints**: `composes` only goes from L1 ProcessGroup to L2 Process, OR from L0 ProcessContext to L1 ProcessGroup. The reverse direction is `part-of` (the derived inverse; view-time only).
>
> **Cardinality**: a ProcessGroup composes 1 or more Business Processes (per MECE-001: every BP is composed by at least one PG); a ProcessContext composes 1 or more ProcessGroups (per the L0L1-CARD-001 cardinality gate). A BP with 0 composers is a MECE-001 finding.
>
> **Deprecated exemption**: MECE-008 exempts `lifecycle_status: deprecated` BPs from the "composed by PG" requirement; deprecated BPs are intentionally not composed (superseded).

**Source rules**: CR-BP-12 (Process Group profile), CR-BP-100 (L0L1-CARD-001 cardinality), MECE-001 / MECE-008. **Verification**: BP-SEM + MECE gates assert the cardinality.

### RT-002: `realizes` (capability realisation)

> **Source / target constraints**: `realizes` only goes from L2 Business Process to a Business Capability (in `dea-catalog-business-capabilities`). The target is in a different catalog; the relationship carries an `identity_scope: dea-catalog-business-capabilities` declaration.
>
> **Cardinality**: a Business Process realizes 0 or more Business Capabilities (a BP may not directly realise any capability, in which case the BP is part of a decomposition that eventually realises). A Capability is realized by 1 or more Business Processes (per the MECE-style coverage gate in BC's conformance).
>
> **Soft-deprecated**: the `capabilities_delivered` field on BP records is soft-deprecated; new records use `relationships[].relationship_type: realizes` (per CR-BP-03A).

**Source rules**: CR-BP-03A (legacy field migration), BC's MECE coverage. **Verification**: BP-SEM-001..014 + BC's per-type gate.

### RT-003: `specializes` (kernel + specialisation)

> **Source / target constraints**: `specializes` only goes from L2 Business Process to L2 Business Process (per the kernel + specialisation discipline, CR-MM-PROC-01 + ADR-0006). The target is a more general BP; the source is a more specific BP.
>
> **Cardinality**: a BP specializes 0 or 1 parent BPs (single inheritance); a BP may be specialized by 0 or more child BPs. Multiple inheritance is not currently supported.
>
> **Discipline**: every BP SHALL have a `process_specialization` field declaring its specialisation; the field is the audit anchor for the `specializes` relationship.

**Source rules**: CR-BP-SPEC-BP-01 (Business Process Specialization), CR-MM-PROC-01, ADR-0006. **Verification**: BP-SPEC-01-001..007.

### RT-004: `part-of` (derived inverse of `composes`)

> **`part-of` is the derived inverse of `composes`**: it is generated at query time, NOT stored on the record. Storing `part-of` on a record is a NON-CONFORMANT finding (duplicate information; per DEA-ID-000 §4: "Structural relationships shall not be inferred solely from filenames, directory paths, naming conventions, or identifier segments when the same information can be represented as governed semantic data").
>
> The metamodel's relationship-type enum does not currently admit `part-of` as a primary type (per CR-BP-12); it is generated by view-time inference from the canonical `composes` direction.

**Source rules**: CR-BP-12 (the `part-of` non-admission), DEA-ID-000 §4 (no implicit relationships). **Verification**: the entity-system umbrella gate (Stage 3-4) asserts no record carries `relationship_type: part-of` in its `relationships[]` block.

## 3. Normative rules

### RS-001 (typed-relationship vocabulary) - blocking

> Every entry in a record's `relationships[]` block SHALL carry a `relationship_type` value from the canonical set: `composes`, `realizes`, `specializes`. The value `part-of` is rejected (per RT-004).
>
> A new relationship type is added by amending this spec; the amendment is a normative change that requires an approved CR. The amendment cites the metamodel's source-of-truth and includes the source / target constraints + cardinality rules for the new type.

**Source rules**: EGV-006 (relationship and reference integrity), CR-BP-12. **Verification**: the entity-system umbrella gate asserts the `relationship_type` is one of the canonical set.

### RS-002 (source / target type constraints) - blocking

> Every relationship entry SHALL satisfy the source / target type constraints declared in §2:
>
> - `composes`: source is `ProcessGroup` or `ProcessContext`; target is `Process` or `ProcessGroup` respectively.
> - `realizes`: source is `Process`; target is in `dea-catalog-business-capabilities` (carries `identity_scope`).
> - `specializes`: source is `Process`; target is `Process` (more general).
>
> A relationship entry whose source / target types do not satisfy the constraints is a NON-CONFORMANT finding.

**Source rules**: EGV-006, the per-type constraints in §2. **Verification**: the entity-system umbrella gate asserts the source / target type constraints.

### RS-003 (cardinality) - blocking

> Every relationship entry SHALL satisfy the cardinality rules declared in §2:
>
> - `composes` (PG -> BP): the PG composes 1+ BPs (per MECE-001).
> - `realizes` (BP -> Capability): the BP realizes 0+ capabilities.
> - `specializes` (BP -> BP): the BP specializes 0 or 1 parent (single inheritance).
>
> Cardinality findings are NON-CONFORMANT under `--strict`; the gate exits 1.

**Source rules**: MECE-001, MECE-008 (deprecated exemption), CR-BP-SPEC-BP-01 (single inheritance). **Verification**: the entity-system umbrella gate asserts the cardinality constraints.

### RS-004 (cross-catalog references) - blocking

> Every cross-catalog relationship entry (e.g. a `realizes` pointing at a `dea-catalog-business-capabilities` id) SHALL carry an `identity_scope` declaration. The `identity_scope` value is the namespace of the target catalog.
>
> The cross-catalog URI form (U-01 in the Gap Register) is **deferred to T8 of the umbrella programme** (Legacy ID reconciliation). Today's references use the target id + scope; URL-based URIs are a future enhancement.
>
> Per EGV-006, references SHALL resolve to valid entities within the declared identity scope. A reference whose target id does not exist in the declared scope is a NON-CONFORMANT finding.

**Source rules**: EGV-006, IS-006 (Identity Standard §2). **Verification**: the entity-system umbrella gate asserts the target id resolves in the declared scope; the cross-catalog resolution registry (Stage 5, CR-BP-124) declares the per-catalog id scope.

### RS-005 (no competing definitions) - blocking

> This catalog SHALL NOT introduce a competing definition of a canonical relationship. If a future relationship type is needed, it SHALL be added by amending this spec (per RS-001) with a citation of the metamodel's source-of-truth.
>
> A "competing definition" includes: a different name for an existing relationship type; a different source / target constraint; a different cardinality rule; a different lifecycle transition.

**Source rules**: EGV-006 (no competing definitions). **Verification**: the entity-system umbrella gate asserts the relationship_type values match the canonical set; per-record validation is delegated to the per-type gates.

## 4. Worked example

A Business Process with structural + semantic + cross-catalog relationships:

```yaml
id: processes:process-manage-customer-relationship-f3n8wb
type: Process
name: Manage Customer Relationship
definition: |
  The L2 Business Process that realises the customer-relationship
  management capability for an enterprise customer base.
lifecycle:
  status: admitted
  admitted_at: '2026-08-15'
  admitted_by: CR-BP-03C
relationships:
  # RT-001: structural composition (this BP is composed by a PG)
  # Note: `composes` is a target-relative verb; the PG's relationships[]
  # entry reads: {relationship_type: composes, target: <this BP id>}.
  # The BP's perspective is "I am composed by a PG", which is the
  # derived `part-of` (view-time only; per RT-004, not stored here).

  # RT-002: capability realisation (cross-catalog)
  - relationship_type: realizes
    target: capabilities:capability-manage-customer-relationship-m6fmyx
    identity_scope: dea-catalog-business-capabilities

  # RT-003: specialisation (kernel + specialisation discipline)
  - relationship_type: specializes
    target: processes:process-manage-stakeholder-relationship-pr-operate
```

This example demonstrates:
- RS-001: every `relationship_type` is from the canonical set.
- RS-002: `realizes` source is `Process`; `specializes` source is `Process`.
- RS-003: cardinality is 0+ for `realizes` and 0+ for `specializes`; the BP may have 0 `realizes` and 0 `specializes` entries without being non-conformant.
- RS-004: the `realizes` relationship carries `identity_scope: dea-catalog-business-capabilities`.
- RS-005: no competing definitions; the relationship_types are the canonical set.

## 5. Acceptance criteria

- AC-RS-01 Every record's `relationships[]` entries carry a `relationship_type` from the canonical set.
- AC-RS-02 No record carries `relationship_type: part-of` (the derived inverse is view-time only).
- AC-RS-03 Every cross-catalog reference carries an `identity_scope` declaration.
- AC-RS-04 The cardinality rules per RT-001..RT-003 are satisfied; the deprecated exemption (MECE-008) is honored.
- AC-RS-05 The cross-catalog resolution registry (Stage 5, CR-BP-124) declares the per-catalog id scope for every cross-catalog reference.

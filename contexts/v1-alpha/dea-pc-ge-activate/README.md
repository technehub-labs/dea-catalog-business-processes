## 2. Formal Definition

The bounded enterprise context for **bringing adopted governance
instruments into force** in the **Governance & Existence** domain.
This context addresses the commencement-stage work of verifying
commencement conditions, promulgating adopted instruments, and
confirming effectiveness. Entry-into-force semantics are universal
in legal-regulatory systems (adoption vs commencement are distinct
events in every jurisdiction's statute and regulation practice) and
in corporate governance (resolutions and charters take effect upon
adoption-plus-commencement conditions).

## 2. Formal Definition

The bounded enterprise context for **withdrawing and retiring
operational assets** in the **Enablement & Operations** domain.
This context addresses the retirement-stage work of isolating,
withdrawing, decommissioning and disposing of technology and
physical assets, and resolving their residual data, value and
obligations. Decommissioning is a mature, universally governed
discipline (ISO 55000 asset end-of-life; APQC asset disposition;
eTOM resource retirement; data center decommissioning practice)
with a stable identity wholly independent of the ECF stage label.

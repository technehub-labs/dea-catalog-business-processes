## 2. Formal Definition

The bounded enterprise context for **activating enterprise
relationships** in the **Party & Relationship** domain where
regulatory obligations (identity verification, beneficial-ownership
resolution, risk classification, onboarding approval) trigger
distinct group-level work before the relationship may commence.
This context addresses the regulated-onboarding dimension of
relationship activation; non-regulated first-time activation remains
covered by the receiving Operate cell (relationship operation
includes habitual onboarding) and is excluded.

## 2. Exclusion mechanics

1. **Source-of-truth check.** Of the 139 canonical BP records at `main @ 390e73e` (v0.4.0), exactly one lacks `metadata.activity_references[]`: `dea:process-develop-governance-strategy`. The record carries `lifecycle_status: deprecated` and `status: deprecated`, with a `migration-target` link to `dea:process-develop-corporate-strategy` (StrategyAndDirection x Conceive).
2. **Exclusion rationale.** Three convergent grounds:
   - **CR-BP-21a split (2026-09-07).** User-approved Option A under CR-BP-20 grounded in `domain-grounding.md` §3.1: "GE authorises but does not direct; Strategy & Direction directs within the authorised frame." `dea:process-develop-corporate-strategy` (S&D x Conceive) is the active successor; this record was retired as a duplicate.
   - **MECE-008 exemption (CR-BP-36).** `scripts/check_mece.py` MECE-008 rule exempts deprecated records from the BP-composed-by-PG gate. The same deactivation logic that exempts a deprecated BP from composition also exempts it from L3 decomposition; adding Activities to a parked duplicate would re-activate it through the back door.
   - **Active sibling already covers L3.** `dea:process-develop-corporate-strategy` carries 4 Activities (deposited under CR-BP-52). Adding 4 more Activities to the deprecated G&E duplicate would create stranded Activities at a coordinate whose semantics are owned by the S&D sibling, polluting the L2-L3 traceability trail at G&E x Conceive.
3. **Surface the rationale on the record.** Append a CR-BP-91 entry to `metadata.change_history[]` of `dea:process-develop-governance-strategy` documenting the deliberate exclusion. Additive metadata-only change; version unchanged per SIV-004 (CR-BP-34d).
4. **No record-count churn.** The exclusion produces no new Activity records; `Activity records: 553`, `Records checked: 740`, and conformance-report `total_records: 788` remain at their post-CR-BP-87 / post-CR-BP-90 baselines. The count-assertion triple in `tests/test_check_activity_model.py`, `tests/test_check_execution_boundary.py`, and `tests/test_architectural_regression.py` is unchanged.

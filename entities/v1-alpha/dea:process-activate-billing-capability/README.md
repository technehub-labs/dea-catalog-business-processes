## 2. Formal Definition

Activate a billing or revenue-recognition capability under the enterprise's regulated-entity approval authority within the dea:pc-fa-activate Cell Charter. The trigger is a regulator-acknowledged accounting or rate framework gating live invoicing or revenue recognition; the outcome is the capability live on the regulated billing record with the supervisor's continuing-oversight relationship active and operational controls running, so that the billing or revenue-recognition capability moves from regulator-acknowledged-but-not-live to regulator-acknowledged-and-live. Distinct from general capability build (Build coordinate) and from steady-state billing (Operate coordinate).

## 1. Executive Summary / Intent

The regulated-billing activation work that moves a billing or
revenue-recognition capability from regulator-acknowledged-but-
not-live to regulator-acknowledged-and-live under the
enterprise's regulated-entity approval authority, with the
regulator's acknowledgement on record, the tariff or pricing
approval preserved, operational controls (segregation of
duties, audit trail, reconciliation) live, and the supervisor's
continuing-oversight relationship active, so that the billing
capability is formally live on the regulated billing record
under the regulator's continuing supervision.

## 1. In a Nutshell (provided)

This is a **Process Context** (level L0), named **Organization and Agent Topology Design**, currently **candidate (yellow)**. It is domain `ao`, lifecycle stage Design. Its canonical ID is `processes:pc-ao-design-a4hq2r`.

This context covers the Agency & Organization area at the Design stage: turning capacity intent into concrete structure. It produces the organization design, the catalogue of roles, the competency framework, and the topology showing how human and automated agents fit together, along with the chains of authority between them. Building the teams themselves is the next context along.

## 2. Why It Matters (provided)

To give the enterprise a workable blueprint of roles, competencies, and agent placement with clear authority, before any capacity is acquired.

## 3. Where You'd Encounter It (provided)

Encountered when a new structure is being prepared or an existing one is redrawn — typically after conception work and before recruitment or provisioning begins.

## 4. Worked Example (provided)

*Example (carried out by a person):* The Agency & Organization Process Owner reviews the draft role catalogue and reporting lines, challenges a duplicated approval step, and approves the topology that shows where automated agents sit alongside each team.

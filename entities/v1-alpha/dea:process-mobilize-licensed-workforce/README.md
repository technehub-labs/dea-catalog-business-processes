## 2. Formal Definition

Mobilize a licensed or regulated workforce under the enterprise regulated-entity approval authority within the dea:pc-ao-activate Cell Charter. The trigger is the enterprise regulated-entity approval for a licensed role; the outcome is the live-readiness activation of the licensed role with the supervisor-controlled scope preserved (signing authority or controlled-function designation), fitness-and-propriety attestation recorded, supervised-training completion preserved, and continuing-supervisor notification activated, so that the licensed individual is live on the regulated personnel record under the regulator continuing supervision.

## 5. Behavior

- **Trigger**: A workforce role whose holders must hold a regulator-issued licence is approved by the enterprise regulated-entity approval authority for live work (banking controlled functions under FCA SMCR; healthcare practitioners under US state licensure; licensed professional services under UK SRA / US bar admissions; aviation crew under FAA / EASA type-rating; insurance producers under NAIC Producer Licensing), and the licensed individual is readied for live work on the regulated personnel record.

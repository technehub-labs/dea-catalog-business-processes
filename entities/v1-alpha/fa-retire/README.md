## 1. Executive Summary / Intent

The regulated-run-off work that moves an enterprise financial
entity (or run-off portfolio) from going-concern operation to a
documented, regulator-supervised, policyholder-priority run-off
with statutory accounting basis (liquidation basis under ASC
205-30, IFRS 17 fulfillment measurement for insurance
liabilities, IFRS 5 for held-for-sale groups), culminating in
solvent exit or orderly wind-down with all policyholder
obligations and statutory liabilities resolved.

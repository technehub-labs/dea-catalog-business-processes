## 1. Executive Summary / Intent

The regulated-operations activation work that moves an operations
capability from regulator-authorized-but-not-live to regulator-
authorized-and-live under the enterprise's regulated-entity
approval authority, with the regulator's authorization on record,
operations manuals and runbooks live, operational controls
(incident management, capacity, security, compliance) live, and
the supervisor's continuing-oversight relationship active, so
that the operations capability is formally live on the regulated
operations record under the regulator's continuing supervision.

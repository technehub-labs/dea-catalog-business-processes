## 2. Formal Definition

Sunset a regulated entity's supervisor-acknowledged-and-live strategic plan under the enterprise's regulated-entity retention policy within the dea:pc-sd-retire Cell Charter. The trigger is a competent regulator's directive or rejection that requires the plan's retirement; the outcome is the plan formally sunset with the regulator directive on record, the replacement plan's status preserved, the retired plan's records retained per the regulated-entity retention policy, and the supervisor's acknowledgement of the sunset recorded. Distinct from strategy improvement (Improve coordinate) and from strategy activation (Activate coordinate, which handles replacement-plan activation).

- **Trigger**: A regulated entity's supervisor-acknowledged-and-live strategic plan must be sunset because a competent regulator has required its retirement (failed stress test triggers plan replacement; bank recovery-plan rejection by the SRB; FDIC directive to retire an inadequate resolution plan; state PUC retraction of an approved strategic plan; NHS England directive to retire a non-viable 5-year strategy).
- **Outcome**: The regulated strategic plan is formally sunset with the regulator directive on record, the replacement plan's status preserved, the retired plan's records retained per the regulated-entity retention policy, and the supervisor's acknowledgement of the sunset recorded.

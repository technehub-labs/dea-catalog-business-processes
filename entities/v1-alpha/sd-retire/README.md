## 1. Executive Summary / Intent

The regulated-strategic-plan sunset work that moves a
supervisor-acknowledged-and-live strategic plan to
regulator-acknowledged-and-sunset under the enterprise's
regulated-entity retention policy, with the regulator directive
on record, the replacement plan's status preserved, the retired
plan's records retained per the regulated-entity retention
policy, and the supervisor's acknowledgement of the sunset
recorded, so that the regulated strategic plan is formally
sunset on the regulated strategic-plan record under the
regulator's acknowledgement and the enterprise's retention
policy.

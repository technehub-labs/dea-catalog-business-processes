## Activity decomposition (CR-BP-79)

| Activity | Cohesion | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Assess Regulator Or Court Order Scope | 9/9 | Activity |
| Reduce Governance To Wind-Down Quorum | 9/9 | Activity |

| Assign Retained-Records Custodianship | 9/9 | Activity |

Each Activity decomposes a bounded constituent of the parent BP's outcome text:

- **Assess Regulator Or Court Order Scope** interprets the binding order, identifies binding obligations and deadlines, and maps them to the governance regime and policy instruments the entity must wind down.
- **Reduce Governance To Wind-Down Quorum** reduces governance bodies to the wind-down quorum required by the controlling statute or regulator or court order, sunsets or supersedes the affected policy instruments, and confirms the reduced governance regime is fit to act under the binding order.
- **Submit Statutory Dissolution Filings** prepares, executes, and records the statutory dissolution filings required by the controlling statute or regulator or court (IRS 501(c)(3) termination, California Secretary of State Form DISS, state Attorney General notifications, court receivership termination reports, FCA authorisation surrender).
- **Assign Retained-Records Custodianship** assigns retained-records custodianship to a designated custodian for the statutory retention period (California Corp Code 6323, FTC/DOJ obligations, IRS recordkeeping under Treas. Reg. 1.6001-1), retains the service-of-process agent, and obtains regulator or court acknowledgement.

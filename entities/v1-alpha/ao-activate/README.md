## 1. Executive Summary / Intent

The licensed-workforce mobilization work that moves individuals
from credentialed-but-not-live to credential-and-inlive under the
enterprise's regulated-entity approval authority, with the
supervisor-controlled scope preserved (signing authority or
controlled-function designation), fitness-and-propriety
attestation recorded, supervised-training completion preserved,
and continuing-supervisor notification activated, so that the
licensed individual is live on the regulated personnel record
under the regulator's continuing supervision.
